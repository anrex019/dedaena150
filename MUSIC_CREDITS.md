# Music sources and licenses

The history door streams **Chakrulo (1957)**, performed by the Georgian State Folk Song and Dance Ensemble, traditional Georgian song. Wikimedia Commons describes the recording as public domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chakrulo_(1957).ogg

The words door streams **Tavisupleba instrumental**, composition by Zacharia Paliashvili, official Georgian national anthem. Wikimedia Commons lists the recording as public domain under the Georgian legal exception for official national symbols: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tavisupleba_instrumental.ogg

The audio is requested directly from Wikimedia Commons; it is not bundled into this repository. These are interim licensed recordings. The second door's recording is orchestral/instrumental, **not piano**. The game and future doors still use the project's original synthesized music pending suitable licensed recordings. No recordings by Niaz Diasamidze or 33a are included.
